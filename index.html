Company Profile

Pharma2Tech Systems

Pharma2Tech Systems has been founded by leading minds at the intersection of Information Technology and Healthcare Sciences. Our leadership brings multi-domain expertise in Pharmacovigilance (PV), Clinical Trials Data Processing, Drug Regulatory Submissions, and Enterprise Healthcare Software Development.

Formed as a dedicated entity to serve the global Life Sciences ecosystem, Pharma2Tech Systems specializes in empowering the next generation of healthcare professionals with technological competency.

About Career Catalyst Program

Empowering Pharmacy Graduates for Corporate IT Excellence

The Career Catalyst Program is an industry-driven initiative by Pharma2Tech Systems designed to convert academic knowledge into practical corporate skills. We train Pharmacy graduates (B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D) in core Healthcare IT domains.

Through real-time case studies, software sandbox simulation, mock interviews, and continuous mentorship, our students master complex workflows like ICSR safety reporting, MedDRA coding, GCP compliance, and database validation.
