Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions asked by students

Who can join your programs?

B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D, and Life Science students/graduates who want to start or switch their career into Pharma IT roles like Pharmacovigilance, CDM, and Regulatory Affairs.

Are the internships online?

Yes, all our virtual internships and training modules are 100% online with live sessions, flexible batch timings, and practical hands-on software guidance.

Will I receive a Certificate?

Yes, upon successful completion of the training or internship, you will receive an ISO Recognized Certificate along with 1-on-1 resume building & interview prep.
